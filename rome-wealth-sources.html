Every Kind of Rich

Your Life at Every Level of Rich in Ancient Rome

Sources and media credits for the October 2026 episode. The protagonist and successful career path are fictional. Ancient authors' wealth figures are reported testimony. Army pay estimates and census thresholds are explained in context; no modern-dollar exchange rate is asserted. Contemporary stock clips and later paintings illustrate the narration and are not records of the historical events.
