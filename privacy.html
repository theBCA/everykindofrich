Privacy Policy

Every Kind of Rich ("we") publishes short documentary videos on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. To do that we run a small private publishing tool (the "Every Kind of Rich publishing tools"). This policy explains what that tool and this website do with data.

Who uses the publishing tool

The tool is used only by the channel owner, to manage the channel owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to, reads from or posts to anyone else's account.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's permission, the tool uploads videos to the owner's channel, sets their title, description, privacy and scheduled publish time, and reads the status and public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos.
  • Instagram (Meta): with the owner's permission, the tool publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account and reads their basic public metrics.
  • TikTok: with the owner's permission, the tool uploads videos to the owner's own TikTok account and reads their basic public metrics.

The tool does not access viewers' personal data, comments' authors' private data, messages, contacts or any account other than the owner's.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens for these services are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's encrypted keychain. Video files, metadata and public performance numbers are stored on the owner's computer. Video files may be placed briefly at a public web address so that a platform can fetch them for publishing; they are videos that are being published publicly anyway.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data obtained from Google, Meta or TikTok APIs is used only to publish and manage the owner's own content and is not transferred to anyone else.

Google API Services

The publishing tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Meta in Instagram settings under "Apps and websites", and TikTok under "Manage app permissions". Revoking access and removing the keychain entries deletes all stored tokens. To ask about or request deletion of any data related to you, email us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
