How we make the videos

Every story is researched from published sources: court records, news reports, auction results and company filings. Each number in a video is tied to a source, and the sources are listed in the YouTube description. Pictures come from public-domain and openly licensed archives such as Wikimedia Commons, credited in each description. Narration uses a synthetic voice; scripts, research and editing are our own.
